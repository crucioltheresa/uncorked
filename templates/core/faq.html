Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Get in touch, we're happy to help.

Do I need to be 18 to order?

Yes. You must be 18 or over to buy alcohol from Uncorked. Our couriers may ask for ID on delivery and won't leave wine with anyone under 18.

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day usually arrive in 1–2 working days in Dublin and 2–4 working days elsewhere in Ireland. See Shipping & Returns for costs.

How can I pay?

We accept Visa, Mastercard and other major debit and credit cards. Payments are handled securely by Stripe, so we never see or store your card details.

What is the sommelier chat?

Tap the chat button in the corner of any page and answer a few quick questions about what you like, what you're eating and your budget. You'll get wine suggestions from our shelves, and you can add them to your cart straight away.

A bottle arrived damaged. What do I do?

Sorry about that! Send us a photo within 48 hours of delivery through our contact form and we'll send a replacement or refund the bottle, your choice.

Do I need an account to order?

No, you can check out as a guest. With an account you can save your delivery details, see your order history, keep a list of favourite wines and write reviews.

Do you offer a discount for bigger orders?

Yes, you get money off when you buy {{ discount_bottles }} bottles or more, and delivery is free over a certain order value. The details are on our Shipping & Returns page.
