About Uncorked

We're a small, independent wine shop in Dublin with one simple idea: every bottle on our shelves should have a story worth telling.

What we pour

Natural wines with a bit of wildness, classics from iconic estates, and plenty in between. We taste everything we sell, and we buy mostly from small, independent growers who farm with care and make wine with as little intervention as they can.

How we choose

No algorithms, no endless aisles. Just a short, well-loved list that changes with the seasons. If a wine doesn't make us want a second glass, it doesn't make the shelf.

Not sure where to start?

That's what we're here for. Take our wine quiz, chat with our sommelier using the button in the corner, or send us a message. We love talking wine.
