Privacy Policy

We only collect what we need to run the shop and get wine to your door, and we never sell your data. Here's the plain-language version.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and password (stored securely, never readable by us), plus any delivery details you save to your profile.
  • Your orders: your name, email, delivery address, Eircode and what you bought, so we can deliver it and keep proper records.
  • The newsletter: your email address, if you sign up.
  • The contact form: your name, email and message, so we can reply.
  • Favourites and reviews: the wines you save and any reviews you write.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never touch our servers. We only receive a confirmation that the payment went through.

How we use it

To process and deliver your orders, send order and account emails, reply to your messages and, if you've subscribed, send our newsletter. We keep order records for as long as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time, and unsubscribe from the newsletter whenever you like. To ask us to delete your data, email {{ contact_email }} from the address you use with us. We'll confirm within 30 days.

Questions

Email us at {{ contact_email }} or use our contact form.
